## How to Contribute

We welcome contributions of all kinds from the open-source community:
1. **Submit Tested TVs**: Have you tested CallOnTV on your television brand? Open a pull request to add your model to the [Hardware Compatibility Table]({{ '/hardware' | relative_url }}).
2. **Improve Scripts**: Submit enhancements to the `/usr/local/bin/tv` CEC controller or autostart scripts.
3. **Report Issues**: Help us identify edge cases by reporting bugs on our [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/callontv/callontv.github.io/issues) tracker.
